2. **Money.** In South Korea the service supports driving rotation only, without money (Passenger Transport Service Act Art. 81 prohibits paid transport in private cars, including expense sharing, outside narrow commute exceptions). Elsewhere a group may enable an actual-cost split calculator; amounts are set by users and the service never processes payments. Users must follow local law.
