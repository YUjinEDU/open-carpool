# Privacy Policy (template)

> This is a **template** for organisations or businesses that operate open-carpool. It is not legal advice. Adapt it to your deployment and have it reviewed before you publish. Fill in the `[brackets]`.

## 1. Data we process
| Category | Data | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Account | display name, email, profile photo URL, sign-in provider id (Google, Kakao, email link) | sign-in provider |
| Group activity | groups, role, can drive, seats, plate last digit (only for groups using a plate-rotation policy), weekly commute schedule | entered by you |
| Approximate area | area label and coordinates rounded to 3 decimals (about 100 m) | entered by you via address search |
| Trip records | date, driver, riders, absences, completion time | created while using the service |

open-carpool **does not collect or share live location.**

## 2. Purposes
Group membership, crew planning and fair driver rotation, trip records and ledger, aggregate statistics and CSV export for group admins, abuse prevention.

## 3. Retention
Account data until you delete your account. Group data until you leave the group or the group is deleted. CSV files already exported by a group admin are controlled by that group.

## 4. Sharing
We do not sell or share your data. Members of the same group see what group operation needs (display name, driving availability, schedule, approximate area, trip records).

## 5. Processors and international transfers
Google LLC (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore) [region]; [hosting provider, e.g. Vercel Inc.]; NAVER Cloud / Google Maps Platform for address search. Where data leaves your jurisdiction, the processors' standard contractual safeguards apply [adjust for GDPR/other laws].

## 6. Your rights
You can access, correct, export or delete your data in the app's profile screen or by contacting [contact]. EU/UK residents may also lodge a complaint with their supervisory authority.

## 7. Security
No passwords are stored by the service (Firebase Authentication). Access is restricted by Firestore security rules (only members of a group can read its data; only you can read your account record). Traffic is encrypted with HTTPS.
